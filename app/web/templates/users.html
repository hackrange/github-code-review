A group is a list of repositories with a name on it. Give somebody the group rather than pasting the list onto their account, and when the project picks up another repository everybody in the group gets it at once. A group replaces the login on the host. A member sees only what their groups cover, whatever their login reaches. Somebody in no group sees only what their login reaches. Administrators see everything.

External sites groups
Who may look at which external sites. These are kept apart from the scanning groups above: a scanning group decides what code somebody sees, and an External sites group decides which sites, so the two can share a name. Give a group its sites on the External sites page, on one site's Edit or with Edit the ticked sites, and put people in it in Edit account. Members can view the sites, their scans and reports; only administrators change or scan them.
