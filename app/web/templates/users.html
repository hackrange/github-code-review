A group is a list of repositories with a name on it. Give somebody the group rather than pasting the list onto their account, and when the project picks up another repository everybody in the group gets it at once. A group replaces the login on the host. A member sees only what their groups cover, whatever their login reaches. Somebody in no group sees only what their login reaches. Administrators see everything.
